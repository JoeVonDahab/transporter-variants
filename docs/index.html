Calibrated evidence for every ClinVar variant in 783 transporter and ion-channel genes

Predictions for all 349,056 variants, from missense to intronic, each reported as an ACMG evidence strength rather than a raw probability. The evidence bins were validated on genes that took no part in calibrating them.

Half of these variants are not missense

Most variant-effect predictors score missense changes only. In these genes, 170,051 ClinVar entries are synonymous, intronic, splice, frameshift, nonsense, indel or UTR variants. AlphaMissense, the strongest missense predictor, covers 177,464 variants here, all of them missense, and scores 23.6% of the labelled subset.

Read the evidence, not the score

Each score is converted to a likelihood ratio against the base rate and binned at the odds behind the ACMG combining rules (Tavtigian et al. 2018): 350 for very strong, 18.7 strong, 4.33 moderate and 2.08 supporting, with the reciprocals for benign evidence.

A 0.5 cut-off on the raw score over-calls badly. The model's training set was 45% pathogenic, while a gene panel is mostly benign, so a raw probability is miscalibrated for that setting. The likelihood ratio is what transfers.

The table shows how pure each bin is when the calibration is refitted on half the genes and read off the other half, so no gene calibrates the bin its own variants fall in. These are the rates to expect on a gene the model was not tuned for.

Before you use it

Exclude variants seen in training

147,162 rows are flagged in_training: the model was fitted on their ClinVar labels. Any accuracy estimate must leave them out. Every figure on this site already does.

Check AlphaMissense coverage on the routed track

Where has_alphamissense is FALSE, AlphaMissense does not cover the variant and the routed track serves the native score. The v2_plus column imputes AlphaMissense there and should not be read.

Mechanism is a hypothesis generator

The mechanism layer, written only for confidently pathogenic variants, transfers across proteins in aggregate. Its per-variant calls on genes without measurements are unproven: on SLC19A2 and SLC19A3, two independent models agreed at Spearman 0.009, against 0.536 where the answer is known.

ClinVar is partly circular

ClinVar assertions are themselves informed in part by computational evidence, so some circularity cannot be excluded from any benchmark built on them. The within-class results are the more conservative reading.

The protein map summarises existing variants

Regional colours average the predictions for ClinVar variants that have been reported near each residue. They are not a saturation scan, and a critical region with few reported variants shows as grey.

Research use

These are predictions, calibrated and validated, but not a clinical classification. Combine them with other evidence under the ACMG framework, as the evidence strengths are designed for.
